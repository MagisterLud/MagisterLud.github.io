We discuss algebraic $n$-valued groups, their multiplication laws in terms of Kronecker sums, and their relation to Wendt matrices. The talk presents joint work with Victor Buchstaber.

[Seminar announcement](https://www.mathnet.ru/php/seminars_all.phtml?ds=1747688400&eventID=9&force=nextm&option_lang=rus&period=tday&wshow=event). See also [arXiv:2505.04296](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.04296).
