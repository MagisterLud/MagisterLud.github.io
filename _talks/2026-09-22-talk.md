We discuss the Buchstaber genus, its associated formal groups, and its relation to the Ochanine, Krichever, and Witten genera. This is the first part of a two-part talk; the continuation was held on 29 September 2026. The complete slides cover both sessions.

[Seminar announcement](https://ms.hse.ru/announcements/1201130824.html). The talk is based on [arXiv:2603.21118](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21118) and the joint preprint with Victor Buchstaber, [arXiv:2609.15878](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15878).
